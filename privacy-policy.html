FireCom Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

FireCom is a mobile app developed by Luke Swain in Perth, Western Australia. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is handled. We aim to comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password when you register. Passwords are handled by our backend provider and are never visible to us in plain text.
  • Incidents: incident names, whether an incident is active, and notes you or your crew add to an incident.
  • Crew (contributor) details: the team join code used to link a contributor to an incident owner, and the contributor's name.
  • Messages: crew messages you send or receive in the app, including the sender's name and the message text.
  • Push notification tokens: a device token so we can send you notifications.
  • Location: see section 5. Your location is not stored on our servers.

FireCom does not include advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools.

2. Why we collect it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, to let incident owners and their crews share incidents, notes and messages, and to send notifications about activity that concerns you. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

3. Where your data is stored

App data is stored with Appwrite Cloud in its Sydney, Australia region. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). If you choose to connect FireCom to your own self-managed Appwrite server, your data is stored on that server instead and you are responsible for it.

4. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers your device with the Expo push notification service, which delivers messages through Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android). We store the resulting token against your account. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

5. Location

With your permission, FireCom uses your device location while the app is open to show a live compass heading and local weather. To get the weather, your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Location is not saved to your account or our database, and is not tracked in the background. You can deny or remove location permission in your device settings; the rest of the app still works.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account, incidents, notes and messages for as long as your account is active. Notification tokens are replaced or removed when they stop being valid or when you sign out. When an account is deleted, its data is removed from our database within 30 days, apart from anything we must keep by law.

7. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days. Under the APPs you may also complain to us, and if you are not satisfied with our response, to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

8. Third parties

  • Appwrite (database, sign-in and storage, Sydney region)
  • Expo, Apple and Google (push notification delivery)
  • Open-Meteo (weather lookup using approximate location)

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We do not share your information with anyone else unless required by law.

9. Children

FireCom is intended for adult fire and emergency crew members. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
